FX Guide.
Light on the ears.

Effects for audiobook voices: your saved presets, gentle recipes for echoes, spirits and gods, and how far to push before a listener notices the effect instead of the story.

Six rules
  • 1
    The voice stays the star. A listener should hear a character, not a plug-in. Keep reverb and echo mixes under about 25%; most of these recipes sit at 12–20%.
  • 2
    Small moves read as acting. ±50 cents sounds like a different person. ±1–2 semitones, a different body. Past ±3, a creature, and only for short lines: hours of it tire the ear.
  • 3
    Same setting, every chapter. Put a character's effects on their own track and Arm them, or save them as a voice preset. Then the voice can't drift between sessions.
  • 4
    Order matters. Armed effects run in the order shown (Armed: A → B). Pitch first, then doubler or chorus, then echo, then reverb last. Re-arming an effect moves it to the end.
  • 5
    Tails fade before the next line. A reverb or echo still ringing under narration muddies it and lifts the room tone. Shorter decay, fewer repeats.
  • 6
    Listen twice. Once on headphones, once on a phone speaker. If you notice the effect before the words, back the mix off by a third.

Using effects

In the Booth: Tools ▸ Effects. Effects work on the track you're on.

On audio already recorded

Open an effect, pick a preset chip or move the sliders, ▶ Preview, then Apply. It changes the selection, or the take you've clicked. Undo brings the original back.

On every new take

⏻ Arm for new takes keeps the effect on the track: each take recorded there gets it when you press Stop, in every chapter. Change a setting and press ↻ Update armed; it reads ✓ Updated once it matches.

Voice presets

＋ Save as preset saves the track's armed effects and its track sound (EQ, compression) under a name. Click the name on any track, in any book, to put that voice there.

Track sound

The track's chain: low cut, EQ, de-ess, compressor, and a limiter at −3.2 dB. Leave the limiter on whenever an effect is armed: it catches the peaks pitch and doubling can add.

Recipes

Arm them in the order listed, on the character's own track. Every number is a starting point: listen, then go lighter rather than heavier.

Echoes, spirits and spooky

Inner voice

Telepathy, thoughts heard aloud, a spell's words

  1. Echo time 180 ms · repeats 15% · mix 18%
  2. Reverb decay 0.8 s · damp 55 · pre 10 ms · mix 12%

One soft repeat says "inside the head" without smearing the words. The built-in Inner voice preset (mix 40%) is for a single line.

Ghost

A spirit, a voice from the other side

  1. Voice doubler mix 25% · spread 70 · delay 30 ms · layer +5 st
  2. Reverb decay 2.0 s · damp 50 · pre 20 ms · mix 18%

A faint higher shadow plus a bit of hall. The built-in Ghostly (mix 65%) is a sound effect; this is a voice.

Dark place

Caves, crypts, a voice down a corridor

  1. Echo time 320 ms · repeats 22% · mix 16%
  2. Reverb decay 2.6 s · damp 20 · pre 28 ms · mix 18%

The Cave preset with the mix cut from 42% to 18%: the space without the wash.

Prophecy

An oracle, an ancient voice, words from a well

  1. Pitch −50 ¢ (Low Lilt)
  2. Echo time 450 ms · repeats 20% · mix 15%
  3. Reverb decay 1.6 s · damp 45 · pre 25 ms · mix 14%

A long, quiet repeat sounds older than a loud one.

Gods and the vast

Voice of a god

Benevolent, enormous, calm

  1. Pitch −1 st
  2. Voice doubler mix 25% · spread 40 · delay 18 ms · layer −12 st
  3. Reverb decay 3.0 s · damp 40 · pre 35 ms · mix 18%

The octave-down layer gives size; keeping it at a quarter keeps your diction on top. Pre-delay lets each word land before the space answers.

Dark god

A demon lord, something old and hungry

  1. Pitch −3 st
  2. Voice doubler mix 30% · spread 50 · delay 16 ms · layer −12 st
  3. Reverb decay 2.3 s · damp 40 · pre 24 ms · mix 16%

Heavier than the god, still readable. Keep the built-in Demon (−8 st) for a single snarl.

Goddess

Ethereal, luminous, a voice like light

  1. Pitch +50 ¢ (High-Lilt)
  2. Chorus depth 25 · speed 1.2× · mix 25%
  3. Reverb decay 2.5 s · damp 35 · pre 30 ms · mix 18%

A gentle shimmer, not a wobble: the built-in Shimmer at about half its mix.

Legion

Many voices as one, a hive, a choir of the dead

  1. Chorus depth 50 · speed 1.6× · mix 35%
  2. Voice doubler mix 30% · spread 80 · delay 24 ms · layer −12 st

Busy by nature: a line or two at a time, then back to a cleaner voice.

When it sounds wrong

Metallic or phasey

Too much pitch, or doubler and chorus together. Take the pitch a step smaller, or drop one of the two.

Words smear

Reverb decay too long or mix too high. Shorten the decay first; then add pre-delay (20–35 ms) so the start of each word stays dry.

The echo distracts

Fewer repeats before less mix. One quiet repeat reads as a place; three read as a gimmick.

Peaks clip

Pitch and doublers add level. Keep the track's limiter on (−3.2 dB), or lower the track a dB or two.

Sounds like a different book

Effects too heavy against plain narration. Halve every mix, then bring back only what you miss.

A voice drifted

It was applied by hand in one chapter and armed differently in another. Save it as a voice preset and use that everywhere.

THE LAMP ROOM  (v0.6: Booth, Studio, Effects, Duet lines & Celebrations)
================================================

Opening it
----------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent (Documents is fine). Keep the files together.
   Replacing an older version: delete the old app folder and put this one in its place.
   Your work is NOT in the app folder, so nothing is lost.
2. Double-click index.html. It must open in Google Chrome (or Edge).
   If it opens in Safari: right-click index.html > Open With > Google Chrome.
3. Bookmark the page in Chrome.

Two folders, two jobs
---------------------
- LIBRARY folder (chosen on first run): books, chapters, sessions, settings. Small files.
  Later this moves into a shared Google Drive folder so you and Jack see the same titles and schedule.
- STUDIO folder (asked the first time you press Record): your recordings, 48 kHz 24-bit WAV.
  Keep it OUTSIDE Google Drive / Dropbox. Audio is big and stays on this computer.

First run asks whose computer this is. The Lamp Room then opens as that person:
their pace, their sessions, their lines in duets. Change it in Settings, or switch
"Reading as" inside the Booth.

Duets
-----
Import a manuscript whose co-narrator lines are highlighted (blues = male, pinks = female,
pale yellow = system voice). The Lamp Room asks who reads each part. In the Booth your lines
are lit, everyone else's are dimmed, and the line just before yours shows as a grey cue.
Follow-along, word counts, pace and pay count only your parts.
A whole chapter in someone else's POV: set the POV column on that chapter in the book view.

Duet lines (Tools ▸ Co-narrator)
--------------------------------
Whoever narrates a chapter is its lead. The other reader records their lines first, as usual.
- Co-narrator: record your lines straight through, a breath between each. Then press "Send my lines".
  Each line is cut out of your recording (by your word timing and the pauses around it) and saved
  in the shared Library folder: Books/<book>/duet/<chapter>/<your name>/. "Send + save a copy"
  also puts them in a folder you choose, if you'd rather share another way.
- Lead: open the chapter and their lines arrive by themselves (or "Check for new lines", or
  "Load from a folder…"). Record straight through. When you finish the line before theirs and go
  quiet, their line drops onto their track and plays in your headphones. Wait for "YOUR TURN".
  C drops their next line in by hand if follow-along misses it.
  Lines not in yet? A slot is marked where each one goes; "Splice all" fills the slots later,
  and places any other missing line by your word timing, opening the gap on every track.
- Each line in the list: play, go to it, take it back out (↩), or Place it at the playhead.

Pickups (Tools ▸ Pickups): every open flag in the chapter. Fix goes back to the start of that
sentence and punches in. ✓ marks it dealt with.

Celebrations
------------
Big glowing words for: each daily goal (words, booth hours, earnings if you set one), every
1,000 words in a sitting, beating your usual pace or your best pace on a chapter, chapter done,
book recorded, and new books. Anything that comes up mid-take waits until you press Stop.
Combo meter (top right of the Booth): every 100 of your own words without a 10-second break
raises the combo through Finished Hour's 20 tiers, Warming Up to Omega. From Overdrive up the
screen joins in. The co-narrator's lines don't break it. Click the meter to hide it.
Switch any of it off in Settings: Celebrations, Combo meter, Confetti, Calm (words only).

Booth & Studio keys  (change any of them: Hotkeys button in the Booth)
-------------------
R          Record / pause / resume. Track 1 carries on from the end of track 1 (or after a later take
           on another track); other tracks punch in at the playhead
1 – 6      While recording: carry the take straight onto that track, no gap. Otherwise: select it
⌥1 – ⌥6    Record on that track from the playhead
F          Flub: back to the start of the sentence, pre-roll, record over it
Shift+R    Punch in at the playhead. The new take replaces only what you re-read; the old take is
           kept under Tools ▸ Takes so you can swap back
E          Apply the last effect again (to the selection, or the take under the playhead)
S          Stop            Space   Play / stop (plays the selection if there is one)
M / N      Flub marker / named marker (named markers go into WAV exports)
⌘X ⌘C ⌘V   Cut / copy / paste (paste pushes everything after it along)
⌘⇧V        Paste over (replaces)
Delete     Delete the selection and close the gap — or, with a take selected, delete that take
⌘A         Select the whole chapter     ⇧Home / ⇧End  Select from the playhead to the start / end
B          Split the take at the playhead
T          Next track      Shift+T  Add a track (up to 6; tracks belong to the whole book)
[ / ]      Shorter / taller tracks (or drag the top edge of the dock)
− / =      Zoom out / in (⌘ + scroll; scroll to pan)
⌘− / ⌘=    Smaller / larger reading text (or A− / A+, or ⌘ + scroll over the text)
Home/End   Playhead to start / end      , / .    Previous / next flag
Shift+P    Open / close the Tools panel
⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z   Undo / redo                  Esc      Stop playback, clear the selection
C          Co-narrator: drop their next line in now (or mark a slot; not recording: at the playhead)

On the waveform: click to place the playhead, drag to select, click a take's label strip to
select the take, drag the strip (or hold ⌥) to move it along or onto another track.

In the text: select words and a little menu asks who reads them (you, your partner, narration).
Use it when a highlight was missed or landed on the wrong person.

Tools panel (left side of the Booth)
------------------------------------
Effects        Pitch, voice doubler, reverb, echo, chorus, robot, phone/radio/muffled.
               Preview, Apply (to the selection, the selected take, or the whole track; Undo
               brings the original back), or Arm on a track so every new take recorded there
               gets it when you press Stop. Each track keeps its own armed effects and settings,
               so track 2 can be a demon at −8 and track 3 a child at +6.
               Save a track's armed effects as a named voice preset and reuse it anywhere.
               The FX button on each track head opens this for that track.
Track sound    A live chain per track (high-pass, EQ, de-esser, compressor, limiter). Off by
               default since Auphonic masters for you.
Takes          Every take in the chapter (play, jump, delete) and the punched-over takes kept for swapping.
Levels         Gain on a selection or take (+/− dB, nothing re-recorded), and an ACX check.
Mic & reading  Reading as, microphone, theme, sans or serif reading text.

The chapter clock works like Finished Hour's: it starts the first time you press Record
on a chapter, keeps running through Stop, playback and punches, pauses after 5 idle
minutes, and stops when you mark the chapter complete. Click it to pause.

Export: WAV 44.1 kHz/16-bit or MP3 192k (ACX), or raw WAV 48 kHz/24-bit for Auphonic.
Files land in <Studio folder>/<Book>/exports/ unless you choose a place.

Follow-along uses Chrome's speech recognition, which needs an internet connection
(Chrome sends the audio to Google to recognise it; your recordings themselves stay local).
If follow-along won't start, click words to move your place, and tell Claude.

# The Lamp Room

A private audiobook booth, studio and planner for Jack & Anne, their editor and invited co-narrators.

Open the app: **https://annemarienarrates.github.io/lamp-room/** in Google Chrome (Edge works too; Safari doesn't).
Bookmark it. Updates arrive on their own: reload the page to pick them up.

## Your work never comes here

This repository holds only the app. Books, recordings, logs and money live in the folders you choose on each computer:

- **Library folder:** books, chapters, people's progress, the planner. Small files. For shared books it sits in a Google Drive folder set to *Available offline*.
- **Studio folder:** your recordings (48 kHz 24-bit WAV) and exports. One per computer, kept **outside** Google Drive and Dropbox.

The first run asks whose computer this is. Sharing a computer? Click the 👤 name at the top right to switch who's recording.

## The guide

- In the app: the **?** in the top bar.
- Printable, in `guide/`: the full Guide, the Keyboard sheet, and Quick Starts for co-narrators and the editor (each has a PC version).

Follow-along uses Chrome's speech recognition, which needs an internet connection. Chrome sends the speech to Google to recognise it; your recordings stay on your computer.
